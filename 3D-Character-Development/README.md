# 3D Character Development

Use this section to showcase character art and your 3D production workflow.

## Suggested gallery

### Final renders
Place polished character images in `media/final-renders/`.

### Wireframes and topology
Place wireframe, retopology, and mesh screenshots in `media/wireframes/`.

### Rigging
Place rig views, skeleton layouts, weight-painting screenshots, and control-rig images in `media/rigging/`.

### Animation
Place animation stills, GIFs, or links to video turntables and motion clips in `media/animation/`.

## For each character
Include a short description, your role, tools used, what you personally created, and any relevant production challenges.

Only upload work you have permission to share. Credit collaborators where appropriate.
